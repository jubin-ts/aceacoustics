What is seamless acoustic plaster?

Seamless acoustic plaster is a sound-absorbing finish applied over an acoustic substrate panel. Once finished, it looks like a smooth plastered ceiling, with no joints, grid or perforations, but it absorbs sound like a high-performance acoustic tile. It can follow curves, domes and vaults, which makes it a favourite with architects designing premium Gulf interiors.

  • Monolithic, joint-free finish on flat and curved surfaces
  • NRC from 0.75 up to 1.05, depending on system
  • Any colour, any size
  • Recycled-content options for green building projects

Frequently asked questions

Is acoustic plaster really seamless?

Yes. Once the plaster is finished, the surface has no visible joints, grid or perforations, so it looks like a normal smooth ceiling while absorbing sound.

Can acoustic plaster be used on domes and curved ceilings?

Yes. DegoSilent Ultra is designed for domes, vaults and curved surfaces.

How well does acoustic plaster absorb sound?

Depending on the system and build-up, DegoSilent acoustic plaster reaches NRC 0.75 to 1.05.

Can acoustic plaster be coloured?

DegoSilent Plus+ is available in all colours. Other systems can be finished to suit your interior palette.

Is acoustic plaster suitable for gyms?

Yes. Seamless plaster ceilings are used in gyms and wellness spaces to control noise from music and equipment while keeping a clean, premium look.
