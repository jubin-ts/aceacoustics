Soundproofing vs acoustic treatment: know the difference

Many soundproofing projects in Dubai fail because the wrong product is used. There are two separate problems:

Soundproofing (sound insulation)Acoustic treatment (absorption)
What it doesStops sound passing through walls, doors, floors and ceilingsReduces echo and reverberation inside a room
Measured bySTC / Rw (higher blocks more)NRC / αw (higher absorbs more)
Typical productsSoundproof doors, dense partitions, seals, resilient flooringAcoustic ceilings, wall panels, baffles, acoustic plaster
SolvesNoisy neighbours, meeting-room privacy, plant-room noise, footstepsEcho, poor speech clarity, noisy restaurants

Most offices, hotels, clinics and studios need both. ACE ACOUSTICS supplies each side, so one partner covers the whole acoustic package.

Frequently asked questions

Do acoustic panels soundproof a room?

No. Acoustic panels absorb echo inside a room. Soundproofing needs mass, airtightness and isolation: dense partitions, sealed acoustic doors and resilient layers.

What STC rating does a meeting room need?

For confidential speech, consultants typically target STC 45–50 or higher for walls and a matched rating for the door. The door and its seals are often the weak point.

Can you reduce footstep noise from the apartment above?

Yes. Impact noise is reduced at the floor with anti-vibration underlays, such as DAIKEN's composite flooring layers, often combined with an acoustic ceiling below.

Do you supply soundproof doors in Dubai?

Yes. We supply DAIKEN soundproof door systems with high-density cores and acoustic seals to projects in Dubai and across the GCC.

Do I need soundproofing or acoustic treatment?

If the problem is noise from another room, you need soundproofing. If the room itself sounds echoey or loud, you need acoustic treatment. Many projects need both, and we can advise.
