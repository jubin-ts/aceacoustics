When do you need acoustic wall panels?

Hard walls of glass, gypsum and stone reflect sound straight back into a room. In meeting rooms, boardrooms, auditoriums, classrooms, gyms and restaurants that reflection causes echo, flutter and poor speech clarity. Acoustic wall panels absorb those reflections at ear height, where the ceiling alone can't reach.

Wall panels also pair well with an acoustic ceiling or baffles. The ceiling controls overall reverberation, and the walls handle reflections between parallel surfaces.

Acoustic panels vs soundproofing

Acoustic wall panels absorb sound inside a room. They don't stop noise passing through a wall into the next room. If you need to block noise between rooms, read our soundproofing guide, which covers DAIKEN high-STC partitions and soundproof doors.

Frequently asked questions

How many acoustic wall panels does a room need?

It depends on room volume, existing finishes and the target reverberation time. As a rule of thumb, meeting rooms often need panels on 15–25% of the wall area alongside an acoustic ceiling. We can calculate coverage from your drawings.

Are fabric acoustic panels fire rated?

Our fabric-wrapped panels use a fiberglass-wool core with Class A/B fire-resistant fabric. Check the data sheet for the exact classification of the fabric you choose.

Can acoustic panels be made in custom colours or prints?

Yes. Fabric panels come in a full colour range, and aluminium-frame systems accept custom woven or printed fabrics for branding and artwork.

Do acoustic wall panels soundproof a room?

No. Panels absorb echo inside a room. To stop sound passing between rooms you need sound insulation, such as high-STC partitions and acoustic doors.
