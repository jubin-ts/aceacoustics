Why choose an acoustic ceiling?

The ceiling is usually the largest open surface in a room, so it does most of the work of controlling echo. A good acoustic ceiling absorbs speech and equipment noise before it bounces back into the space. The result is a calmer office, a classroom where students hear the teacher, and a restaurant where guests do not have to shout.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best acoustic ceiling tile for an office in Dubai?

For open-plan offices we usually recommend fiberglass or stonewool tiles with NRC 0.85 or higher and a Class A1/A2 fire rating. Where you also need to stop sound travelling between rooms, a DAIKEN EXCELTONE MN tile (CAC 32) is a good choice.

What does NRC mean?

NRC (Noise Reduction Coefficient) is the share of sound a material absorbs, on a scale from 0 to 1. NRC 0.85 means the ceiling absorbs about 85% of the sound that hits it.

Are your acoustic ceiling tiles suitable for humid areas?

DEGOFON stonewool and fiberglass tiles stay stable up to 99% relative humidity, so they suit the Gulf climate. Mineral wool tiles are rated to 70% RH and are better kept for conditioned, dry spaces.

Do you supply acoustic ceilings in Saudi Arabia and Qatar?

Yes. We supply projects across the GCC, including Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Doha, Kuwait, Bahrain and Muscat, from our base in Dubai.
