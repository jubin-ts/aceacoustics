Baffles and clouds: acoustics for open ceilings

Exposed soffits and industrial-style ceilings are popular across Dubai and the GCC, but bare concrete reflects almost all sound. Acoustic baffles are vertical panels hung in rows. Because sound reaches both faces, a small ceiling area gives a lot of absorption. Acoustic clouds are horizontal panels hung below the slab, often used as a design feature over desks, tables or reception counters.

PET felt baffles vs fiberglass baffles

Many GCC tenders specify PET acoustic baffles, made from compressed polyester felt that is often recycled from plastic bottles. PET is light, comes in many colours and is easy to cut. Before you lock it into a specification, compare the following:

PET felt bafflesFiberglass-wool baffles (DECOACOUSTIC)
Core materialPolyester (PET) fibreGlass wool, a mineral fibre
Typical fire performanceUsually Euroclass B or C (some products quote ASTM E84 Class A, which is a different test)Non-combustible mineral core. DECOACOUSTIC clouds are rated A2
Sound absorptionVaries a lot with thickness and density (often NRC 0.4–0.9)NRC 0.85 at 30–50 mm
Heat & humidityCan sag or deform in hot, unconditioned spacesDimensionally stable, rated to high humidity
LookFelt texture, many coloursSmooth painted or fabric finish, custom colours
SustainabilityRecycled PET contentHigh recycled glass content, recyclable

PET figures are typical market ranges, not one product. Always compare the data sheets for the products being tendered.

Where the fire strategy, a basement, a car park, an atrium or a sprinklered escape route calls for non-combustible materials, a fiberglass baffle is often the safer approved alternative to PET. If your drawings specify PET baffles, send them over. We'll tell you honestly whether a DECOACOUSTIC baffle meets the design intent and quote it as an alternative.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between acoustic baffles and clouds?

Baffles hang vertically in rows and absorb sound on both faces. Clouds hang horizontally like floating panels. Baffles give more absorption per square metre of ceiling, and clouds work well as a design feature over specific zones.

Are PET acoustic baffles fire rated?

Many PET felt baffles reach Euroclass B or C. Some quote ASTM E84 Class A, a different test standard. Fiberglass-wool baffles use a non-combustible mineral core. Always check the fire classification your consultant or civil defence authority needs.

How far apart should acoustic baffles be spaced?

Spacing typically ranges from 300 to 600 mm, depending on baffle depth and how much absorption the room needs. Closer spacing gives more absorption. We can calculate the layout for your space.

Can you supply an alternative to the PET baffles in my specification?

Yes. Send us the specification and drawings and we'll propose a DECOACOUSTIC fiberglass baffle with matching or better acoustic and fire performance for approval.

Do baffles block access to services above the ceiling?

No. Baffles and clouds leave the soffit open, so MEP services, sprinklers and lighting stay accessible.
